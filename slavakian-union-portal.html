ABOUT THE SLAVAKIAN UNION
An overview of our nation, identity, and values.

Welcome to the Slavakian Union

The Slavakian Union is a sovereign, democratic republic established on 21 November 2024 following its historic declaration of independence. Stretching from the Baltic coast to the Arctic frontier, the Union is a modern European state built on resilience, innovation, and cultural diversity.

Today, Slavakia is one of the most dynamic and rapidly developing nations in the region, committed to security, stability, and prosperity for all who call it home.

Government & Political System

Type of Government: Presidential Republic
Legislature: Bicameral Congress

  • National Assembly (Lower Chamber) — 110 members
  • Council of Provinces (Upper Chamber) — 26 members

The President serves as Head of State and Government, Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces, and guardian of the Constitution.

Core national principles include:

  • Rule of law
  • Democratic representation
  • Accountability
  • Civilian control of the military
  • Protection of individual rights

Provinces & Territories

The Slavakian Union consists of 13 provinces, each with its own local administration:

  • Baltics
  • Pskov
  • Novgorod
  • St. Petersburg
  • Karelia
  • Murmansk
  • Vologda
  • Arkhangelsk
  • Nenets
  • Komi Republic
  • Yamalia

The nation spans 1,026,199 square miles of territory, including vast forests, arctic tundra, and rich coastal regions.

Population & Society

Population: ~96.6 million
Primary Language: Slavakian
Main Religion: Protestant (with full religious freedom guaranteed)

Slavakia is known for:

  • Strong community values
  • Diverse cultural heritage
  • High literacy and education rates
  • Robust social programs
  • A forward-looking, innovation-driven economy

The Union embraces immigrants, valuing those who contribute to national growth, security, and prosperity.

Economy

GDP: Approximately $11.04 trillion
Currency: Euro (€)

Economic strengths include:

  • Advanced manufacturing
  • Energy production
  • Technology and aerospace
  • Defense industries
  • Shipping and logistics
  • Natural resources (forests, arctic minerals, fisheries)

National Defense

The Slavakian Union maintains one of Europe's most capable defense structures, ensuring territorial integrity, allied cooperation, air and maritime security, nuclear deterrence, and peacekeeping operations.

Armed Forces: Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Coast Guard, Space Force, Missile Forces, and the National Guard.

National Values

The Union was founded on a promise of freedom, sovereignty, and equal opportunity. Its Constitution affirms:

  • Civil liberties
  • Human rights
  • Democratic governance
  • Freedom of expression
  • Equality under the law
  • Protection for workers and families

Why People Choose the Slavakian Union

  • Strong social safety nets
  • Economic opportunity
  • National stability
  • World-class infrastructure
  • Universal healthcare
  • Public safety and low corruption
  • Educational advancement
Welcome to a Nation Built on Strength and Hope
Whether you are a citizen, resident, visitor, or new arrival, the Slavakian Union invites you to take part in its ongoing story — one defined by resilience, freedom, and opportunity.
